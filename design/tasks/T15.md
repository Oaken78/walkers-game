# T15 Descent camera: pitch floor on steep ground behind the walker

Pillar served: 2 "Legs that read" (the walker never vanishes behind an edge); 3 "Venture and return" (the trip back
down from the talus pocket stays readable)
GDD refs: section 6 "On a descent, where the ground behind the walker rises steeper than about 25 deg, the camera
holds a pitch floor of that slope minus 5 deg (about 35 deg on the 40 deg talus), so the edge behind never hides the
walker. It eases in and out; on flatter ground the camera returns to the player's own pitch."; section 10 rule 1
"Seen from behind, every foot is also clear of the chassis and the ground"; Decisions log 2026-10-09 (Klas).
Tier: 3
Implementer: visuals-dev
Branch: (filled by /dispatch)

## Goal
Walking down the talus with the camera behind at the player's usual pitch of 20, the camera lifts to the floor before
the shelf edge can hide the walker, then settles back to the player's pitch on flat ground. The player's own pitch is
never lost: the floor only raises the shown pitch while it applies.

## Work items
1. **Slope behind:** measure the ground rise between the walker and the camera (for example a few downward rays
   along the line from the target to the camera), as an angle in degrees. Expose it read-only.
2. **Floor:** while that angle exceeds 25 deg, the shown pitch is at least (angle - 5), capped at `pitch_max_deg`.
   Ease toward the floor and back with an exported time (start at 0.3 s). The player's pitch (`pitch_deg`) is kept;
   only the shown pitch is raised. Mouse input still moves `pitch_deg`, and above the floor it shows as usual.
3. **Unit tests:** the floor maths (no floor at <= 25 deg, floor = angle - 5 above it, cap, ease), and that
   `pitch_deg` is unchanged by the floor.
4. **Scenario `gait_camera`:** Crawler descending the talus with the camera behind at player pitch 20, shots at the
   shelf edge, mid-face (along_z about -16) and the foot (about -14.5). Log the shown pitch, the slope behind,
   FOOTBOX and FOOTVIS for each. Assert at mid-face and foot: shown pitch >= 30 and every pad FOOTVIS 5/5. Then walk
   onto the flat apron: assert the shown pitch is back within 1 deg of 20 within 1 s.
5. **Confirm the number:** report the lowest shown pitch at which every pad is 5/5 at mid-face and foot. If it is not
   about 35, report the measured value; the lead updates the GDD.

## Review round 2 decisions (2026-10-09, GDD Decisions log)
Blocking (code-reviewer and playtest-critic): the floor lifts the camera at every boulder, ledge, block and wall
(`slope_from_heights` keeps the steepest single sample, so any rise over 0.35 m at 0.75 m triggers it).
1. **Sustained slopes only:** count a slope only where at least 2 consecutive 0.75 m segments each rise steeper than
   25 deg, and the total rise exceeds 1.2 m (taller than any step-up). The slope is the mean over that steep run.
   Unit tests: one steep step between flat samples gives no floor; a 40 deg ramp gives 40.
2. **Evidence in gait_camera:** CAMSTATE every 10 frames for a Strider and a Scout through the boulder strip (6 s),
   down the ledges lane, and off the 0.8 m pocket block. Assert the largest lift <= 3 deg in each. Mid-face and foot
   still show >= 33 with every pad FOOTVIS 5/5 (logged).
Also in this round:
3. **Look ahead:** also sample the ground ahead along the camera's forward line (0.75 and 1.5 m); a sustained drop
   ahead counts like a rise behind. Edge shot: lift >= 10 and every pad 5/5.
4. **Ease:** smoothstep in and out, about 0.45 s up and 0.8 s down per 20 deg (exported). Ease the floor itself and
   show max(pitch_deg, eased floor), so mouse moves above the floor show at once (code-reviewer 4).
5. **Return assert:** time it from the root crossing onto the apron; within 1 deg of the player's pitch within 1.5 s.
6. **Reach:** sample out to the camera's horizontal distance, not a fixed 5.25 m; the arm stays >= 7.9 from flat+20f to
   flat+100f.
7. **Aim:** the floor lets go while `aim` is held (lead decision, consistent with recentring).
8. **snap():** recompute the floor from a fresh measurement (no stale lift after a teleport).
9. **Guards and tests:** clamp `slope_sample_spacing` (>= 0.1); drive the rig's own `_ease_floor` in a unit test.
Waived: the FOOTVIS assert stays a log line; T13 adds a `min_foot_vis` helper to gait_course.gd and the assert lands
after both merge. Baselines: re-approve gait_rig__crawler_descend_p20 (intended); the gait_camera shots after this
round.

## Review round 3 decisions (2026-10-09, GDD Decisions log)
Blocking (code-reviewer; the playtest-critic saw the same pop): the floor target jumps 2-3 deg in one frame every
0.75 m down the face (the mean includes the partial segment at the corner or edge).
1. **Steady floor:** use the steepest full segment of the qualifying run (not the mean), and ease the target itself
   (at most about 20 deg/s). Track the largest frame-to-frame change of `shown_pitch_deg` while the floor is active and
   assert <= 0.5 deg per frame over the whole descent; a unit test for a 40 -> 37.9 -> 40 measurement sequence.
Also this round:
2. **Sight-line trigger:** replace `floor_max_start_m` with a sight-line test: a qualifying slope (behind or ahead)
   triggers the floor only if its top stands above the line from the walker's lowest foot to the camera at the
   player's pitch. Evidence: a Crawler standing 1.5, 2.0 and 2.6 m from the talus foot, camera behind and over the
   face at player pitch 17 and 20: floor active and every pad FOOTVIS 5/5; release FOOTVIS logged at +60/+70/+80.
3. **Look-ahead NaN:** a missed ahead ray must fall back to `-base`, not `base`; unit test with a NaN sample.
4. **Asserts where the decisions say:** the return assert about 90 frames (1.5 s) after the root crosses onto the
   apron; `arm_min_m` asserted again after the last release step.
5. **Evidence routes:** the ledge runs spawn with offsets that reach decks 4-15 (0.50-1.05 m); log the round-1
   single-sample angle beside the new measure so a run proves the old rule would have tripped; note the Scout runs
   that stop early (the boulders, the 0.8 m block) as not exercised.
6. **For Klas (evidence only):** two more mid-face shots at player pitch 45 and 50 (camera behind), with FOOTBOX, so
