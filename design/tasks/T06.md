## Goal
The Scout's cannon points where the walker faces and tips up and down with the camera. A small dot at the screen
centre shows where you look, and an orange ring in the world shows where the shot will land. The ring merges with
the dot when you are on target, and it slides off toward an edge chevron when you look away. Shots fly at 60 m/s,
spread with the legs, and hit a 0.6 m target exactly where the dot is when the target sits on the heading. Two risks
are checked with stand-in targets before the drone exists: can every build turn onto a target fast enough (risk 8),
and can the dot reach a target hovering close and high (risk 9).

## Work items
1. **Damage contract (scripts/combat/):** keep it small, because three later tasks build on it.
   - `Health` holds `max_hp` and `hp`, with `damage(amount)`, `repair_full()` and the signals `changed` and
     `depleted`.
   - `Hurtbox` is an Area3D with `take_hit(damage: float, source: Node, point: Vector3)` and a `hit_taken` signal.
     Enemy hurtboxes sit on layer 3. The player's hurtbox is a chassis-sized box on layer 2 that follows
     `WalkerBody.body_pose()`, so shots between the legs miss (plan note).
   - Unit tests in test_combat.gd.
2. **Aim maths (`AimMath`, pure, in scripts/weapons/):** GDD 8.3 exactly:
   - P is the camera's centre-ray hit on layers 1 and 3 (never the player) up to 120 m, or the point at 120 m.
   - d is the horizontal range from the body origin to P, clamped to 4-120 m; h is P's height.
   - Q lies d along the heading at height h.
   - Each weapon's elevation is the angle from its muzzle to Q, clamped to -10..+45 deg world-relative, and slews at
     360 deg/s. Its yaw is the heading (lateral convergence only from the socket offset).
   - d and h hold while the camera yaw is more than 90 deg off the heading.
   - Unit tests in test_aim_math.gd, including a body tilted 39 deg.
3. **Pulse cannon and projectiles:**
   - 4 shots/s, 15 damage, 60 m/s, spread = the build's `spread` stat (deg), drawn from a RandomNumberGenerator
     seeded per weapon.
   - LMB (`fire`) fires every mounted cannon in even phases: weapon k of n fires k / (n x rate) s after the first
     (two cannons: every 0.125 s, so gaps alternate 7 and 8 ticks at 60 Hz).
   - Projectiles are pooled. At most 40 live: fire is refused at the cap and the refusals are counted.
   - Projectiles use layer 4 with mask 1 and 3, and live 2 s (120 m). Damage is applied once per projectile, even
     where hurtboxes overlap. Each shot leaves the muzzle of the barrel as drawn on that tick.
   - Projectiles do not inherit the walker's velocity (GDD 8.3): a strafing or turning walker hits where the
     reticle shows.
4. **Mount (`WeaponRig`):** a node given a WalkerBody and an OrbitCamera.
   - It reads `get_build()` for the top sockets that hold a pulse cannon and places one elevating barrel per cannon
     on the walker's drawn pose (`body_pose()`).
   - It hides the walker's own static barrel meshes (the `Tops` children the walker draws for cannons) and re-mounts
     on `build_applied`.
   - Logic runs in `_physics_process`. Anything drawn per frame follows the interpolated pose, with no 60 Hz
     stepping (T17).
   - Report the walker API you would rather have (for example a top-socket mount transform and a flag to skip
     drawing cannons).
5. **Aim marks (ui/aim/):** GDD 12 numbers at 1080p, scaled with resolution:
   - a 6 px camera dot;
   - a 28 px ring with a 3 px stroke in `#FF8A3D` and a 2 px ink outline, at the projection of the first hit along
     the line from the weapons to Q, from the same tick's weapon pose;
   - a 5 px stroke over an enemy hurtbox;
   - a merge into a 4 px pip within 12 px of the dot;
   - a dashed ring (8 segments) at an elevation limit;
   - a 32 px edge chevron, 24 px inset, when the ring is off screen or behind the camera;
   - `mouse_filter = IGNORE` on every Control.
   - Grayscale check with `tools/pixels.ps1`: the ring's ink outline differs from the background beside it by
     >= 0.25 luma on a sky sample and on a ground sample.
6. **Aim-up range:** `pitch_min_deg` becomes -20. Below -10 the spring arm shortens against the ground (rock wins).
   - At -20 on flat ground, the arm length and the chassis's screen position are reported.
   - The chassis stays in frame.
   - Every existing camera shot stays SAME; any DIFF is explained.
7. **Test range (aim_range):**
   - Flat ground with a reference build (`WalkerBuild.scout()`, `.strider()`, `.crawler()`), the OrbitCamera, the
     WeaponRig and the aim marks.
   - Stand-in targets with a 0.6 m sphere hurtbox on layer 3, 45 HP and a 0.06 s white flash on a hit.
   - Scenario helpers for scripted input (turn, aim, fire, mouse motion).
8. **Risk 8, heading speed only:** for the Scout, Strider and Crawler, with no `aim` held, bring the heading onto a
   target 90 deg off. Assert <= 1.0 s (GDD: the Crawler about 0.93 s). The moving-drone tracker check belongs to T07
   (Klas, 2026-10-09: keep the drone work light for now).
9. **Risk 9 spike (aim up):** camera pitch -20, a target 12 m out on the heading and 6 m up. The dot sits on it, a shot
   hits it, and the chassis is in frame.
10. **Cost:** the WeaponRig plus aim maths at most 0.3 ms per tick p99. 40 live projectiles at most 1.0 ms per tick
   p99. Wall-clock asserts use p99 <= 3x the bound and keep deterministic counts exact (T16 lesson).

## Acceptance checks
- **Unit:**
  - fire rate cap;
  - damage once per projectile;
  - yaw equals the heading within 0.1 deg;
  - elevation clamp on a 39 deg tilt;
  - d clamps to 4 m with the camera looking straight down;
  - d and h hold beyond 90 deg off the heading;
  - two cannons fire 0.125 s apart on average;
  - 40-projectile cap;
  - Health and Hurtbox signals.
- **aim_range asserts:**
  - weapon yaw error <= 0.1 deg on every tick;
  - with a target on the heading inside the limits, every shot passes within 0.5 deg of P (spread 0 for this check);
  - two-cannon gaps of 7 or 8 ticks, mean 0.125 s +/- 0.01;
  - the risk 8 heading times;
  - the risk 9 hit.
- **Shots (new):**
  - `aim_range__on_target` (merged pip);
  - `aim_range__reticle_offset` (heading 30 deg off the target: dot and ring apart);
  - `aim_range__limit` (dashed ring);
  - `aim_range__chevron` (camera looking behind);
  - `aim_range__aim_up` (pitch -20, target 12 m out and 6 m up under the dot, chassis in frame).
- Every existing shot stays SAME, or its DIFF is explained.
- `tools/verify.ps1 -Game walkers--<branch> -Tier 3` PASS after `import.ps1 -Force`.
- Any removed or loosened check listed (old -> new, why).
