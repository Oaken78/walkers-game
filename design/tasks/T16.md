## Goal
At a ledge a walker's front legs reach up and plant on the top, the nose pitches up, the body hauls itself over, and
the other legs hang briefly and follow. Too tall a face and the front legs paw at it. Going down mirrors it, and a
walker never climbs what it cannot descend. The Strider climbs 1.44 m, the Scout 0.90 m, the Crawler 0.54 m; only
the Strider gets into the 1.2 m ledge pocket.

## Work items
1. **Climb stat:** `climb` = 0.9 x mean reach in walker_build.gd, with unit tests (Scout 0.90, Strider 1.44, Crawler
   0.54) and the contrast (A - B) / A = 0.625.
2. **Reach:** a front-row leg whose stride target is blocked by a rise may plant on the top up to `climb` above its
   current foot: within 0.95 x reach of its hip, knee bend >= 0.05 x reach, inside grip, far enough past the edge
   that the shin clears the corner (>= 0.15 m; the pose check saw shins clip by up to 4 cm at exactly 0.15). The
   reach swing lasts 1.5 x step time and rises to edge + 0.15 x reach before moving over the lip. The climb height
   is a hard check (+ 0.03 m tolerance): the pose check found the Scout's legs could plant on the 1.2 m top.
3. **Haul:** with both front feet on top the body pitches nose-up up to min(grip - 5, 25), rises at most 1.5 m/s,
   advances at most 0.4 x top speed until the last foot is up. Follow-on legs step up to `climb` once their hip is past
   the edge. Move the T13 wall probe (`_wall_probe_height`) to feet + climb + 1 cm.
4. **Hang and paw:** a leg with no valid foothold lifts within 1 tick to 0.25 x reach, >= 0.05 m clear, paws on a
   0.6 s cycle, counts as airborne. Planted legs never fewer than leg count minus the gait limit. The centre of mass
   stays inside the planted feet's polygon by >= 0.1 x mean reach (it shortens a move like the reach limit). No leg
   hangs or reaches up on ordinary ground (bumps, boulders within stride step-up).
5. **Step-down:** at an edge the body (chassis centre) lowers up to 0.32 x reach and pitches nose-down up to
   min(grip - 5, 25); front legs plant up to `climb` below; following legs step down as their hips pass the edge. A
   drop deeper than `climb`: front legs hang over it and the body stops.
6. **Pad overlap (T13 item 10, carried over):** the wave gait's lead (about 1 m at speed) exceeds the hip spacing, so
   one side's pads interpenetrate (min_pad_gap_m about -0.3). Propose a gait-level fix inside walker_body.gd (for
   example a lead cap per row) and report its speed cost per build; do not trade away more than 5 % of any build's
   flat speed without the lead's approval.
7. **Map:** `LEDGE_RISE` 1.2 in valley_layout.gd; test_valley_geometry follows; valley_pockets: only the Strider stands
   on the ledge pocket floor (it reaches and hauls), the Scout paws at the face and recovers; every build that climbs
   into a pocket climbs back out.
