## Binding notes
plan.md "T12 wiring notes from the T06, T08 and T09 reviews" are part of this packet (workshop setup and exit, economy
signals, combat, drones). In short: the workshop owns its walker, camera, environment and lights, so instance it on
entry and free it on exit; `Economy.banked` (also emitted for 0) drives `Health.repair_full()`, `DroneField.respawn_all()`
and the node refill; set the field walker's max HP from the build's stats on every exit.

## Flow
1. **Boot:** the workshop with the Scout build, 0 banked scrap, the catalog's starting inventory.
2. **Exit (Tab, valid build):** the field walker takes the build (`apply_build`), spawns at the workshop pad facing
   down-valley, input on, mouse captured, field camera current.
3. **Enter (F within 4 m of the bench):** bank (repair, drones and ring 1-2 nodes refill), then the workshop visit.
4. **Death (HP 0):** input off, collector off, hurtbox off; `collapse(1.0)` (legs fold, chassis settles to the ground);
   fade 0.5 s; `Economy.die(position)`; respawn as a workshop visit (the bank of 0 repairs and refills, as T08 built it);
   the HUD toast "Wreck cache: N scrap at <distance> m" after the fade.
5. **Recall (hold F 3 s):** `Economy.recall(position)`, fade 0.5 s, workshop visit. No collapse.
6. **Reclaim:** walking onto the cache reclaims it (T08 collector); a second death before that destroys it.
7. **Pause (Esc):** pauses the tree, releases the mouse, a plain "Paused" label; Esc again resumes and recaptures.

  death, recall, a second death destroys the cache, no transition while fading); test_walker_body.gd: `collapse`
  takes 1.0 s, ends with the chassis on the ground and input off, and a later `apply_build` stands the walker up.

  route and bank; a part is bought and socketed; expedition 3 dies (a forced damage call at a set point is fine), the

  0 log errors, 0 orphans. Logs: scrap per expedition, seconds per expedition, seconds from the pad to the first drone
