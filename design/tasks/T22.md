## Goal
The player points with the mouse and every gun on the roof swings to the dot by itself. A light gun is there almost
at once; the 40 kg pulse cannon takes half a second for a quarter turn, and turning the body toward the target
gets it there sooner. Each gun has its own ring that shows where its shot goes right now, trailing the dot while
it swings. When the dot is somewhere a gun cannot reach (down at the walker's own feet), that gun's ring stops at
the edge of its reach, turns gray and dashed, and the gun stays silent. Keys only drive the body; aiming with RMB
no longer slows the turn.

## Work items
1. **Aim maths (scripts/weapons/aim_math.gd, pure):**
   - Keep P exactly as today (centre ray, layers 1 and 3, never the player, 120 m, the point at 120 m with no hit).
   - Retire the convergence point Q, the 4-120 m range clamp, the world-relative -10..+45 limits, the 360 deg/s slew
     and the 90 deg hold. Remove their tests, and list each removed test with the new test that replaces it.
   - Add `mount_arc(face)` (top: yaw unlimited, pitch -20..+75 deg in the chassis frame; the front/back/left/right
     and bottom rows of GDD 8.3 as data only, unused in M0) and `traverse_rate(mass_kg)` = clamp(7200 / m, 45, 720).
   - A pure solve: from a pivot and the chassis basis, the yaw and pitch that put the line on P; whether that pose
     is inside the arc (0.5 deg tolerance); the nearest reachable pose when it is not; a P within 1.5 m of the pivot
     holds the current pose, gray.
   - A pure step: each axis rate-limited on its own, chassis-relative, no ramp; a 360 deg mount takes the shortest
     way round; a limited mount never swings through its dead zone.
2. **WeaponRig:** per weapon, keep a chassis-relative yaw and pitch and a live / gray state.
   - Each physics tick: solve, step toward the target pose (or the nearest reachable pose when gray), and place the
     barrel on the drawn chassis pose. The body's own turn carries the gun (so A/D toward P shortens the swing).
   - Fire: LMB fires every live weapon on its own cooldown, along its current barrel line, even mid-swing. Phases
     are fixed by mount order (top_0, top_1, top_2): weapon k of n fires k / (n x rate) s after the first. A gray
     weapon skips its slots and the others keep theirs.
   - Projectiles: unchanged (no inherited velocity, layer 4, mask 1 and 3, pool cap 40, damage once).
   - Expose per weapon what the marks and tests need, for example `weapon_state(i)` -> {live, aim_point (the first
     hit along the current barrel line, layers 1 and 3, up to 120 m), on_enemy, flash_left} and the yaw and pitch.
     Drawn things follow the interpolated pose (T17); logic stays in `_physics_process`.
3. **Aim marks (ui/aim/):** GDD 12 numbers at 1080p, scaled with resolution.
   - The 6 px camera dot at the screen centre, always drawn, on top of the rings.
   - One 28 px ring per mounted weapon (0-3) at the projection of that weapon's `aim_point`, accent `#FF8A3D`,
     3 px stroke (5 px over an enemy hurtbox), 2 px ink outline. A settled live weapon's ring is centred on the dot.
   - Gray: `#646464`, dashed (8 segments, about 50 % duty), ink outline kept, at the arc-limit point; back to solid
     accent on the tick P returns inside the arc.
   - Hit flash: the ring of the weapon that fired the hitting shot flashes `#E6E1D6` for 0.06 s.
   - One 32 px chevron per off-screen or behind-camera ring, 24 px inset, in its ring's colour.
   - The merge pip goes. `mouse_filter = IGNORE` on every Control.
4. **Controls:**
   - `aim_turn_factor` goes to 1.0 or away (RMB no longer slows the turn).
   - `aim` held: mouse 0.10 deg/px (FOV 70 -> 50 as today).
   - Recentring pauses while `fire` is held too, and its timer restarts on release.
5. **aim_range rework:** stand-in targets as today. Add the GDD 8.3 Tests as scenario asserts:
