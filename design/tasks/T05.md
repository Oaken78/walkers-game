## Goal
The M0 world as a greybox: a 400 m valley between cliff walls, the workshop bench at its head, a winding dry wash
down to ruins that close the far end, bumpy floor patches and boulders, a 0.8 m ledge pocket and a 40 deg talus
pocket, and markers for the workshop, scrap nodes and drone sites. Flat palette colours, one sun, sky and fog. Other
packets instance `scenes/world/valley.tscn` and get collision, markers and a wash path to place gameplay on. Nothing
walks here yet: T03 and T12 do that later, so every rule a walker will test is measured here by raycasts instead.

## Fixed frame (every packet relies on these)
- 1 unit = 1 m. `%WorkshopSite` is at the origin, on a flat pad at y = 0, facing down-valley.
- Down-valley is +Z. The head wall is behind the workshop (-Z). Looking down-valley from the workshop, the ledge
  pocket is on the right-hand wall (-X, the GDD's west) and the talus pocket on the left-hand wall (+X, east). A
  top-down view with -Z at the top of the screen matches the GDD 9.1 diagram.
- "Distance" always means horizontal distance from `%WorkshopSite`. Rings: 0 is 0-60 m, 1 is 60-150 m, 2 is
  150-250 m.
- Footprint about x -200..200, z -50..350. The layout is authored data with fixed numbers (noise only roughens the
  bumpy patches, with a fixed seed): the same valley every run. No random worlds (GDD 15).

## Layout rules
| Element | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | About 200 m wide, at least 150 m at every z from the bench to the ruins. Overall fall down-valley 0-10 deg. Every floor surface, wash banks included, at most 28 deg | The Strider grips 30 deg; 2 deg margin so every build can cross the whole floor |
| Bumpy patches | At least 2 patches on or beside the wash with peak slopes of 20-28 deg | The gait show (GDD 9.1 "Bumpy patches up to 30 deg") |
| Workshop bench | Flat pad at least 24 x 24 m, at most 3 deg, centred on the origin, 20-40 m from the head wall, joined to the floor by ground of at most 20 deg | T09/T12 put the workshop on it |
| Dry wash | A winding riverbed from the pad to the ruins, at most 1.0 m deep. Its centreline is a `Path3D` named `%WashPath`, points on the floor surface, starting within 10 m of the workshop site and ending within 15 m of the ruins | The main route; T12's scenarios follow it |
| Cliff walls | Along the whole floor edge (side walls, head wall, the ruins line, and the walls around both pockets): the ground rises at least 30 m within 17.3 m horizontally (60 deg on average), and no surface between 1.0 m and 30 m above the floor is flatter than 60 deg | The map bounds: no foothold above the 1.0 m max step-up, no invisible walls |
| Boulders | At least 20, 0.3-1.0 m tall, with steep sides (step-up content, not ramps), in clusters. None within 4 m of the wash centreline (an 8 m clear lane) and none within 15 m of either pocket entrance | The Crawler (0.36 m) goes around, the Strider (0.96 m) steps over; no staircase into a pocket |
| Ledge pocket | Right-hand wall (-X), entrance centre 160-240 m out. Alcove at least 15 m wide and 12 m deep, floor flat (at most 10 deg) and 0.75-0.85 m above the apron along the whole entrance. The ledge face is vertical (at least 80 deg). A flat apron at least 10 m deep in front of it (height varies at most 0.1 m). Cliff everywhere else around the pocket | Scout step-up 0.60 < 0.8 < Strider 0.96; a bump in front would turn 0.8 m into two small steps |
| Talus pocket | Left-hand wall (+X), entrance centre 150-220 m out. A talus face at least 10 m wide, every surface on it 38-42 deg, rising at least 4 m to a flat shelf. No lip over 0.3 m where the apron meets the slope or the slope meets the shelf. Flat apron in front, cliff everywhere else | Only the Crawler grips 45 deg (Scout 35, Strider 30); 4 m so the Strider cannot step onto the shelf |
| Far end | Ruins (#93806E) half buried in a dune bank. The floor ends at the ruins between 270 and 300 m out. The dune bank in front counts as floor (at most 28 deg); the ruins and the cliff behind them meet the cliff rule, with no gap a walker fits through | The M1 gate; it must hold in M0 |
| Smoke column | 330-370 m out, behind the ruins, at least 80 m tall, smoke colour, no collision. A ray from (0, 3, 0) to a point 60 m up the column hits nothing on layer 1 | The down-valley landmark, visible from the workshop |

A heightmap cannot make a vertical step: a 0.8 m rise across one 1-2 m cell is a 22-39 deg ramp the Scout walks up.
Build the ledge face and the pocket floor (and boulders and ruins) as separate bodies with real vertical faces.

## Markers (T07, T08 and T12 read these after `_ready`)
- `ScrapSite` (`class_name ScrapSite extends Marker3D`, `@export var amount: int`, `ring: int`, `respawns: bool`,
  `in_pocket: bool`) in group `scrap_sites`. Node names are unique: T08 stores depleted ids by node name.
- `DroneSite` (`class_name DroneSite extends Marker3D`, `@export var min_drones: int`, `max_drones: int`,
  `ring: int`) in group `drone_sites`.
- `%LedgePocket` and `%TalusPocket`: `Area3D` covering each pocket floor, collision layer 7 (triggers), mask 2
  (player). T12's bounds and loop scenarios use them to tell "inside the pocket".
- Every marker sits on the floor surface (at most 0.2 m above the collision), and its `ring` matches its distance.

| Ring | Scrap sites | Amount each | Respawns | Drone sites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (0-60 m) | 2 | 5 | false | none |
| 1 (60-150 m) | 3 | 10 | true | 1 site of 1-2 drones at 130-148 m |
| 2 (150-250 m) | 3, plus 1 in each pocket | 20; pockets 60 | true | 2-3 drones at 180-200 m and 2-3 at 225-245 m; 1 drone (min = max = 1) within 25 m of the ledge entrance, on the floor |

- At least 3 scrap sites lie within 8 m of the wash centreline, and consecutive ones are 70-110 m apart measured
  along `%WashPath` (a node every 15-25 s at the Scout's 4.5 m/s). The other sites fill the gaps 20-70 m off the
  wash. The three wash drone sites lie within 15 m of the wash centreline.

## Collision
- Terrain, cliffs, boulders, the ledge, the talus and the ruins are `StaticBody3D` on layer 1 (world) only. Foot
  raycasts mask world only (GDD 13).
- `HeightMapShape3D` or a `ConcavePolygonShape3D` from the same mesh both work; the test raycasts whatever is built.

## Look (first pass; T11 and M1 refine it)
- Placeholder `StandardMaterial3D` per role, saved in assets/world/, with the GDD 10 world palette: ground
  `#CAB294`, ground streaks `#B99F82`, rock base `#9B7D69`, far rock `#A58B78`, ruins `#93806E`, smoke `#57514D`,
  boulders as foreground rocks `#352D28`.
- Nothing in the world uses salvage cyan, threat magenta-red or player orange (GDD 10: those hues are reserved).
- `WorldEnvironment` in `valley.tscn`: sky gradient top `#90A092`, horizon `#DACFB6`; distance fog in the horizon
  colour at 14 % at 100 m and 30 % at 300 m. Plain exponential fog that hits 14 % at 100 m gives 36 % at 300 m, so
  use depth fog (begin, end, curve) and judge it in the screenshot. One `DirectionalLight3D` sun with shadows. No
  glow (there are no emissives yet).
- Draw calls for the world alone at most 300 in the `from_workshop` view (the budget is 1000; the rest is headroom
  for walkers, drones and the M1 outline). Merge or MultiMesh the boulders if needed.
- Building the valley at load takes at most 1.0 s headless. If a 1 m terrain grid is too slow, use 2 m: the cliff
  and pocket rules are what count.

  `{"assert_no_errors": true}`.

  polygon including the pocket alcoves, with the two pocket entrances flagged), the wash points, pocket shapes and
  site list are authored data in `scripts/world/`; the builder and this test read the same data, and the test checks
  the built collision against it.
