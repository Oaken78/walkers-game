## Goal
A self-contained field HUD scene that T12 drops into the game with one `bind` call. At a glance the player knows
how hurt the walker is, what they carry and have banked, which way home and the wreck cache are, when F will enter
the workshop, and how far a recall hold has come.

## API (T12 calls this; keep it this small)
- `bind(health: Health, economy: Economy, walker: Node3D, camera: Camera3D, home: Vector3, recall: RecallHold)`
- `show_toast(text: String, seconds: float = 4.0)`
- `set_enter_prompt(visible: bool)` (T12 owns the 4 m test)
- `unbind()` before the walker or economy is freed. The HUD reads nothing else and survives a null bind.

## Design
- **HP bar, bottom left:** chassis HP of max HP, with the number. It changes on `Health.changed` only. Below 25 % it
  reads as urgent by value and shape (for example a pulse or a notch), never by the threat hue.
- **Scrap, top right:** carried and banked as two labelled numbers; carried is the one at risk, so it reads first.
  Salvage cyan only on the scrap icon.
- **Compass, top centre:** a strip with a workshop mark and, while a cache exists, a cache mark (from
  `Economy.cache_position` and `cache_changed`), each with its distance in metres, relative to the camera's yaw.
  The compass is second on the plan's cut list: build it, but keep it cheap.
- **Prompts:** "F  Workshop" while `set_enter_prompt(true)`. While F is held, the recall progress
  (`RecallHold.progress`) as a small ring or bar labelled "Recall".
- **Toast:** centre-low, fades in and out, one at a time.
- **Layout rules:** total HUD area <= 8 % of the screen at 1280x720 and 1920x1080 (sum of the visible element rects);
  nothing within 40 px of the bottom-right chevron area or the screen centre's aim marks; `mouse_filter = IGNORE`
  on every Control. Text and marks read in grayscale (>= 0.3 luma contrast against what is behind them; a 60 %
  `#14161A` plate where needed, as the F9 readout does).

  sits at the strip's right end; the toast queue shows one at a time; `unbind` disconnects every signal; total HUD
  area <= 8 % at both resolutions; every Control's `mouse_filter` is IGNORE.
