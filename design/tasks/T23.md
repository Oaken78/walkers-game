## Goal
Every plant kicks up a soft, sandy cloud that bursts out low around the pad, drifts and settles over most of a
second, and fades into the ground instead of popping as a white blob. A heavier foot kicks up more: the Crawler's
puffs are visibly bigger than the long-legged Strider's. The contact print from T19 stays as it is.

## Design (GDD 10 rule 1, as written)
- *Size from weight per leg:* w = build `mass` / leg count (`WalkerBuild.stats()["mass"]`, `leg_count()`, read from
  the walker's `get_build()`; never written). Puff width W = clamp(0.5 m x w / 50 kg, 0.25, 1.0) m across; height
  0.5 x W. Scout 315 / 6 = 52.5 kg -> 0.53 m; Strider 285 / 6 = 47.5 kg -> 0.48 m; Crawler 535 / 8 = 66.9 kg ->
  0.67 m (1.41x the Strider). Recompute on `build_applied`.
- *Soft, not round:* each puff is 5 soft sprites at seeded offsets within 0.3 x W of the pad, each 0.5 x W across,
  with a radial alpha falloff: at 0.9 of a sprite's radius the alpha is <= 0.1 x its centre's, and 0 at the rim.
  No single disc, no hard edge.
- *Ground-tinted, never white:* colour `#D9C7AE` (the ground `#CAB294` family: hue 33 +/- 5 deg, saturation
  0.15-0.30), lit by the scene, so it darkens in shadow with the ground. At its peak a puff lifts the floor's
  grayscale luma by 0.03-0.12; no puff pixel has luma above 0.85, or saturation below 0.08 with luma above 0.75.
- *Low opacity:* peak alpha 0.45, reached within 0.08 s of the plant, <= 0.25 by 0.4 s, 0 at the end of life.
- *Drift and settle:* life 0.8 s +/- 1 tick. The sprites burst outward at 0.8 m/s, slowing to 0 by 0.4 s; the puff
  rises to its full height by 0.3 s, then sinks by 30 % while it fades. It stays in the world and does not follow
  the walker.
- *Cost:* <= 2 draw calls per walker for all its puffs (one MultiMesh or one particle emitter); the pool covers the
  Crawler at top speed (about 29 plants/s x 0.8 s = 23 live puffs, x 5 sprites) with no allocation after `_ready`.
  Seeded RNG, so shots and counts are repeatable.
- Keep the quiet start after `build_applied`, the counters the foot_fx scenario reads, and the decal pool as built.

## Acceptance checks
- **Unit (test_foot_fx.gd):** W for the Scout, Strider and Crawler within 0.005 m, plus the floor (25 kg per leg and
  below -> 0.25 m) and the cap (100 kg per leg and above -> 1.0 m); W recomputed after `build_applied`; the alpha
  curve at 0.08 s (0.45), 0.4 s (<= 0.25) and 0.8 s (0); life 0.8 s +/- 1 tick; 5 sprites per puff inside
  0.3 x W of the pad; the sprite texture's falloff (<= 0.1 x centre at 0.9 radius, 0 at the rim); no node created
  after warm-up; the pool reuses the oldest at capacity. Update the T19 tests that assumed 0.6 m, 0.2 s and alpha
  1.0, and list each one (old -> new).
- **Scenario foot_fx.json:** the existing asserts hold (puffs == plants, decal life, pool bounds, 0 orphans, ledge
  normals); add the Crawler at top speed: peak live puffs reported and <= pool size; draw calls for the puffs
  reported (<= 2 per walker).
- **Shots:** `foot_fx__dust_weight` (the Strider and the Crawler mid-walk on the valley floor, side by side or one
  after the other at the same camera); check with `tools/pixels.ps1`: the puff lifts the floor's luma by 0.03-0.12,
  no pixel above luma 0.85, and the measured Crawler / Strider puff width ratio >= 1.3. Re-take `foot_fx__plant`,
  `foot_fx__trail` and `foot_fx__gray` and list them as CHANGED with one line each; every other shot that shows dust
  is listed the same way.
- Tier 3 budgets hold in every scenario (frame p95, draw calls).
- `tools/import.ps1 -Game walkers--<branch> -Force`, then `tools/verify.ps1 -Game walkers--<branch> -Tier 3` PASS.
  Any removed or loosened check listed (old -> new, why).
