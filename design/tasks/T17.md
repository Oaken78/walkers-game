## Goal
Klas played the Windows release build (main 1a777b6) and reports: "The view is stuttering or shaking when any of the
Walkers move. I notice some of the same effect when standing still but rotating the view with the mouse." Our tests
render at a fixed 60 fps, the same rate as the physics tick, so they cannot see uneven motion that only shows when
the display runs faster or slower than 60 Hz or when frame pacing is uneven. Find the cause with numbers, fix it,
and give Klas an on-screen frame-time readout.

## Work items
1. **Reproduce:** run the free-play camera at render rates other than 60, using `--fixed-fps` (or a rendered-frame
   step) of 120, 144 and 165, plus an uneven-pacing case (alternating long and short frames), through
   `tools/shots.ps1` or `tools/smoke.ps1`. Two cases: (a) a walker walking straight on flat ground with the camera
   following; (b) the walker standing still while the yaw turns at a steady mouse rate (inject equal
   `InputEventMouseMotion` steps every rendered frame).
2. **Measure per rendered frame:** the camera's position and yaw/pitch change, and the rendered (interpolated)
   positions of the walker root, chassis and one foot. Report the step-to-step variation (for example the
   coefficient of variation of the per-frame step, and the largest step against the mean) for each part and rate.
   A smooth part has steps that follow time; a stuttering one shows zero steps followed by double steps (60 Hz
   stepping), or steps that jump with frame time.
3. **Fix** what the numbers point to inside the owned paths (for example a camera input or follow path that only
   updates on physics ticks, an arm cast or descent-floor sample that flickers per frame, a smoothing that is not
   frame-rate independent). Assert in camera_smooth: for (a) and (b) at 144 fps, the camera's per-frame step varies by
   at most 10 % from its mean (excluding the start), and the arm length changes by at most 1 cm per frame on flat
   ground.
4. **Frame-time readout for Klas:** a toggle key (F9) shows fps, frame time (current, p95 and max over the last
   2 s) and physics time per frame in a corner label. It is off by default, shows in any build, and does not
   change any shot when off.
5. **Report** the root cause in plain terms and what the walker side looks like in the same measurements, so the lead
   can tell whether T16 also needs a change.

## Review round 2 decisions (2026-10-09)
Klas confirmed the stutter was Windows multi-monitor composition (plan note). The camera checks stay because
high-refresh players need them to be real.
Blocking (code-reviewer 1): `Engine.time_scale` does not emulate a fast display. With `--fixed-fps 60` and 60 Hz
physics every probe frame still gets exactly one tick at a fixed interpolation fraction, so interpolation is never
exercised and the camera_smooth asserts prove nothing about tick/frame mismatch.
1. Emulate a rate R by also setting `Engine.physics_ticks_per_second = 60 * 60 / R` (nearest integer; each tick then
   lasts 1/60 s of game time) next to the time scale. Uneven pacing must vary the tick phase, not just scale both.
   Log ticks per frame and `Engine.get_physics_interpolation_fraction()`: a valid 144 case shows about 0.42 ticks per
   frame and a fraction that walks through 0..1. Re-run the table; keep the camera_smooth asserts on the valid
   emulation. If the camera steps, fix it; if the walker does, report it for T16.
Also this round:
2. DevHarness `process_mode = PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`, so F9/F10/F11 work while paused (test: pause, tap F9, the readout
   exists).
3. The readout shows script physics time and the full physics time (`Performance.TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS`), labelled.
4. F11 restores the previous window mode.
5. Seed the probe's RandomNumberGenerator (1234).
6. Readout (playtest-critic): a 60 % #14161A back plate; move it to the top-left (GDD 12 puts scrap top-right, HP
   bottom-left); a line with the share of frames that got 0, 1 and 2 physics ticks over the last 2 s; a "hitches over
   25 ms since F9" counter; a fixed-sample mode for the scenario so `camera_smooth__frame_readout` can be baselined.
   Fix the literal `\n` in the dev_harness.gd doc comment.
