## Goal
A drone hovers 3-6 m up at its site. When it sees the walker within 35 m it closes in and orbits at 12-18 m. Every
1.5 s it stops, glows magenta-red for 0.6 s and fires a slow bolt at where the walker is. A strafing walker dodges the
bolt. A walker facing the glow lands three pulses and the drone falls and drops scrap. Leave it behind and it drifts
back home.

## Work items
1. **`DroneBrain` (pure RefCounted):** the GDD 8.4 states: idle-hover, patrol, alert, strafe, wind-up, fire, recover,
   dead.
   - Transitions are pure functions of the inputs: player distance, line of sight, time, HP, and distance from the
     spawn point.
   - Sight: the player within 35 m with line of sight.
   - Leash: the player more than 60 m from the drone's spawn point sends it home.
   - Shot cycle: every 1.5 s. It brakes to 0 within 0.1 s at the start of the wind-up and holds still (hover bob
     <= 0.1 m) through the 0.6 s wind-up, the shot and the 0.3 s recovery, then regains orbit speed over 0.3 s.
   - Orbit at <= 6 m/s.
   - A hit never cancels a shot.
2. **Encounter:** a node that owns a site's drones and keeps their wind-up starts >= 0.4 s apart. A global cap of 4
   active drones.
3. **Drone scene:**
   - A ring silhouette with a hurtbox of radius 0.6 m on layer 3, 45 HP (T06 `Health` and `Hurtbox`).
   - Wind-up glow: threat hue `#E8345A`, emissive 0 to 3 over 0.6 s.
   - A 0.06 s white flash on a hit.
   - Dead: falls and drops 3-8 scrap as loose pickups, from a seeded RandomNumberGenerator.
4. **Bolt:** 25 m/s, 10 damage, on layer 5 with mask 1 and 2. It is aimed at the walker's chassis position at fire
   time, with no lead, and damages the `PlayerHurtbox` once. Pool the bolts.
5. **API for T12:** spawning at the valley's DroneSite markers (min to max drones per site), `respawn_all()` to call
   on `Economy.banked`, and a signal per death with its position.
6. **drone_fight scenario** (flat ground, one encounter, scripted input):
   - the brain's states and timings;
   - the telegraph >= 0.6 s before every shot;
   - three Scout pulses kill a drone;
   - a strafing Scout (Q/E alternating) takes <= 40 % of the damage an idle Scout takes over 20 s against 2 drones;
   - the risk 8 spike as GDD 16 states it: every reference build turns onto a drone 90 deg off in <= 1.0 s, and with
     the Scout and with the Crawler a no-lead tracker hits >= 80 % of the shots fired during holds and kills within
     two holds;
   - leash and return home;
   - scrap dropped equals scrap collected;
   - 0 log errors.
7. **Cost:** 4 drones plus their bolts at most 0.5 ms per tick p99. The wall-clock assert uses p99 <= 3x the bound;
   counts stay exact.
