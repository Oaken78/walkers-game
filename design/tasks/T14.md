## Work items
1. **Rig:** put `scenes/camera/orbit_camera.tscn` into gait_course as the play camera. Its target is the walker's
   un-bobbed anchor (body position and yaw without the bob offset). The scripted shot cameras (follow, pair,
   side-on, crest) stay as they are, so the five T03 baselines stay SAME.
2. **Steer modes:** a key toggles `WalkerBody.SteerMode` TANK <-> CAMERA_YAW, with `yaw_source` set to the orbit
   camera. Show the active mode in a small on-screen label.
3. **Free play:** run without a scenario (F6 in the editor), the course starts in free play: tank controls, orbit
   camera, mouse captured (Esc releases). Number keys 1-5 swap Scout, Strider, Crawler, quad and short pair; F1-F4
   (or keys of your choice, listed in the label) teleport to the flat lane, the bumps, the slopes/talus and the
   ledges. Scenario runs are unchanged.
4. **Pitch shots:** `gait_course__pitch_low` (pitch -10) and `gait_course__pitch_high` (pitch 60), 8 m, Scout
   mid-stride on the bumps, through the orbit camera. Log every foot's screen box (like PADS) and assert each is in
   frame and >= 12 px tall scaled to 1080p. Add one steep check: the Crawler tilted 30-39 deg on the talus face,
   camera behind and above (pitch 45-60); assert its uphill feet are in frame and >= 12 px (plan note: they tuck
   under the chassis). Report all foot boxes.
5. **Camera feel numbers (report, do not tune):** the critic suggests `start_pitch` 12-15 deg facing down a lane;
   with the 1.5 m `target_offset` the Crawler's camera sits about 0.47 m above ground at pitch -10. Report the
   camera height above ground at pitch -10 for the Crawler, Scout and Strider, and the bob left on the camera
   (peak-to-peak of the camera position at a steady walk).
