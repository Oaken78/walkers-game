## Goal
The two pieces of maths that make the walk read, as pure classes with no nodes and no physics, so the rules behind
Pillar 2 (feet never slide, never too many legs in the air, a clear alternating rhythm, an instant first step) are
proven by unit tests before T03 puts them on a body. `TwoBoneIK` places a knee and a foot for a hip and a target.
`GaitSolver` decides every physics tick which legs lift, how far through its swing each foot is, and which legs are
blocked. T03 owns the raycasts, the body and the meshes, and feeds this packet's classes.

## Two GDD contradictions, resolved (logged in the GDD Decisions log on 2026-10-08)
1. Section 8.2 says legs split into "two alternating groups (tripod for 6+ legs, wave for 4-5)", but section 5 caps
   4 legs at 1 airborne. Section 5 wins: a wave gait is one leg per group, cycling. Only the tripod has two groups.
2. Handing the turn to the next group at 85 % of the step would put 6 of 6 legs in the air. The airborne limit is a
   hard rule (Pillar 2, M0 acceptance criterion 2). The 85 % rule only passes the turn early; the next group's legs
   still lift only while the airborne count stays within the limit. With full tripods this means the next group
   lifts on the tick the last foot of the previous group plants.

## Contract (T03 and T11 code against these names; do not rename)

### `TwoBoneIK` (`class_name TwoBoneIK extends RefCounted`)
- Inner `class Solution extends RefCounted` with `var knee: Vector3`, `var foot: Vector3`, `var reached: bool`.
- `static func solve(hip: Vector3, target: Vector3, upper: float, lower: float, pole: Vector3) -> Solution`
  - reach = upper + lower. `pole` is a direction (T03 passes outward + up for a spider leg), not a point.
  - Distance hip to target > 0.99 x reach: the foot goes on the hip-to-target line at 0.99 x reach, `reached`
    false.
  - Distance < |upper - lower| + 0.01 x reach: the foot goes on the same line at that minimum distance (straight
    down from the hip when target == hip), `reached` false.
  - Otherwise the foot is the target and `reached` is true.
  - The knee is exactly `upper` from the hip and `lower` from the foot, in the plane of hip, foot and pole, on the
    pole side of the hip-to-foot line.
  - Pole parallel to hip-to-foot: use a stable fallback perpendicular (for example world up, or world forward if up
    is also parallel). Never return NaN or INF.

### `GaitSolver` (`class_name GaitSolver extends RefCounted`)
```
signal step_started(leg: int)   # a foot left the ground (T03 starts the swing)
signal foot_planted(leg: int)   # a foot landed (T11 hangs the dust puff and decal on this)
enum LegState { PLANTED, SWINGING, HOVERING }

# Tunables, defaults from GDD section 5. Public vars, not consts: T03 copies its @export values in before the
# first update, so the T03/T04 gate can tune them without editing this file.
var trigger_ratio: float = 0.5      # a leg wants to step when foot error > trigger_ratio x reach
var step_time_idle: float = 0.30    # step duration at speed_ratio 0
var step_time_top: float = 0.18     # step duration at speed_ratio 1
var handover_progress: float = 0.85
var hover_delay: float = 0.5        # seconds blocked before a leg hovers at its rest pose
var lift_ratio: float = 0.25        # lift height = lift_ratio x reach

func _init(sides: PackedInt32Array, reaches: PackedFloat32Array) -> void
```
- Legs are indexed 0..n-1 in the order T03 passes them. `sides[i]` is -1 (left) or +1 (right). Within one side, a
  lower index means further forward (T01's `mounted_legs()` order satisfies this, but do not depend on T01).
- Groups:
  - 6 or more legs, tripod, 2 groups: a leg's group = (its position among its side's legs counted from the front,
    plus 0 for left or 1 for right) mod 2. 6 legs: group 0 = {L0, L2, R1}, group 1 = {L1, R0, R2}. 8 legs:
    {L0, L2, R1, R3} and {L1, L3, R0, R2}. Gait limit = n / 2.
  - 4 or 5 legs, wave, one leg per group, in this order: left side rear to front, then right side rear to front
    (4 legs: L1, L0, R1, R0). Gait limit = 1.
  - Fewer than 4 legs behaves as wave (no M0 build walks like this; it must not crash).
- `update(delta: float, moving: bool, speed_ratio: float, foot_errors: PackedFloat32Array, targets_valid: Array[bool]) -> void`
  - `foot_errors[i]`: metres from leg i's planted foot to its current target (T03 measures it).
  - `targets_valid[i]`: false when the target is too high, too steep, missing, or out of IK reach.
  - `moving`: true while the player gives any move, strafe or turn input.
  - `speed_ratio`: how fast the foot targets move relative to top speed, clamped to 0..1.
  - Array sizes must equal `leg_count()`; a mismatch is a caller bug: `push_error` and return unchanged.
- Rules applied by `update`, in this order:
  1. **Advance:** each SWINGING leg adds `delta / duration` to its progress, where `duration` was fixed at lift time
     from `step_duration(speed_ratio)`. At progress >= 1 it becomes PLANTED and `foot_planted` fires once.
  2. **Wants to step:** a PLANTED or HOVERING leg wants to step when its error > `trigger_ratio` x reach, or when
     this is the start kick (rule 5). A HOVERING leg with a valid target always wants to step (it never plants
     in place at its rest pose).
  3. **Blocked and hover:** a leg that wants to step but whose target is invalid is blocked (`is_blocked` true;
     T03 stops the body on that side). Blocked time adds up while this holds and resets to 0 otherwise. At
     `hover_delay` the leg becomes HOVERING (it lifts to its rest pose and counts as airborne), but only while
     `airborne_count() < gait_limit()`; otherwise it stays PLANTED and blocked.
  4. **Turn:** the active group keeps the turn while any of its legs is SWINGING with progress <
     `handover_progress`, or wants to step with a valid target (whether it lifts this tick or is held by the airborne limit; rule 4
     runs before rule 6, so this is what keeps the start kick on the active group). Otherwise the
     turn passes to the next group (tripod: 0, 1, 0, ...; wave: the order above). Blocked and HOVERING legs never
     hold the turn, so one stuck leg cannot stall the gait.
  5. **Start kick:** on the first update where `moving` is true after an update where it was false (and on the very
     first update if `moving` is true), the kick arms. While it is armed, every leg of the active group wants to step
     regardless of its error. It disarms when any leg lifts or `moving` turns false, so a blocked first group
     hands the kick to the next group on the next tick instead of losing it.
  6. **Lift:** legs of the active group that want to step and have a valid target lift in order of largest error
     first (ties: lower index), each only while `airborne_count() < gait_limit()`. A HOVERING leg with a valid target
     lifts on that tick whichever group is active: it is already airborne and needs no free slot, and the turn must
     never lock it out (with rule 4, a wave gait would otherwise freeze for good). A lifted leg becomes SWINGING
     with progress 0 and `step_started` fires once.
- Queries: `leg_count()`, `gait_limit()`, `group_count()`, `group_of(leg)`, `active_group()`, `state_of(leg)`,
  `swing_progress(leg)` (0 when not swinging), `is_blocked(leg)`, `airborne_count()` (SWINGING + HOVERING),
  `reset()` (every leg PLANTED, group 0 active, timers 0, used after respawn or recall).
- Helpers:
  - `step_duration(speed_ratio: float) -> float`: lerp(step_time_idle, step_time_top, clamp(speed_ratio, 0, 1)).
  - `lift_height(reach: float) -> float`: lift_ratio x reach.
  - `static func swing_point(from: Vector3, to: Vector3, t: float, lift: float) -> Vector3`: horizontal travel eased
    with smoothstep, plus `Vector3.UP * lift * sin(PI * t)`; t clamped to 0..1. Equals `from` at 0 and `to` at 1.
  - `static func target_lead(velocity: Vector3, duration: float) -> Vector3`: velocity x duration x 0.5 (GDD 8.2
    foot target offset).
  - `static func is_target_valid(rise: float, normal: Vector3, step_up: float, max_slope_deg: float) -> bool`:
    false when rise (target height minus the foot's current height) > step_up, or the angle between `normal` and
    world up > max_slope_deg.
- No allocations per `update` beyond what the arguments need: it runs 8 legs at 60 Hz.

## Out of scope
- Raycasts, foot target placement, the body height and tilt plane fit, stopping the body on a blocked side, leg
  meshes and their bone split (T03).
- Dust puff and contact decal on `foot_planted` (T11); footstep audio (M1); replanting after an external push (M1).
- Idle tidy-up steps while standing still: not in the GDD. If T03 needs them, that is a follow-up packet.
- Heads-up for T03, not to solve here: at the GDD numbers a 4-leg wave gait keeps each foot planted for 3 swings
  (0.54 s at top speed). A 4-medium-leg build at its 3.1 m/s top speed then carries a planted foot about 1.7 m,
  more than its 1.0 m reach. That is why the step timings are public tunables.
