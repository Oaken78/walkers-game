## Goal
Scrap lies at the valley's scrap sites. Walk within 2.5 m and it flies to the chassis in 0.3 s. Carried scrap is at
risk: die (or hold F for 3 s to recall) and it drops as a wreck cache where you stood, which you can reclaim once. Bank
it and it is safe, and the nodes in rings 1-2 refill. No sequence of pickups, banks, deaths, recalls and reclaims ever
creates or duplicates scrap.

## Work items
1. **`Economy` (pure RefCounted, class_name):**
   - **State:** `carried`, `banked`, the cache (position and amount, or none), depleted node names, and `spent` and
     `lost` counters.
   - **Methods:**
     - `pick_up(node_name, amount)`;
     - `bank() -> int`: moves carried to banked; refills the depleted nodes whose site respawns, never ring 0;
     - `die(position)`: carried becomes the cache; an older cache is destroyed and its amount counted in `lost`;
     - `recall(position)`: the same as `die` for scrap;
     - `reclaim()`: once, cache to carried;
     - `can_afford(price)` and `spend(price) -> bool`.
   - **Signals:** `changed`, `banked(amount)`, `cache_changed`.
   - **Conservation:** `carried + banked + cache + spent + lost == total picked up` at every step.
   - Repair on bank belongs to whoever owns the player's Health (T06 builds it, T12 wires it), so emit `banked` and
     do not reach for HP.
2. **Scrap node pickup (scenes/pickups/):**
   - One per ScrapSite, holding the site's `amount`, on layer 6.
   - At least 0.5 m tall, or with a vertical beam, so pocket nodes read from below the lip.
   - Depleted means hidden and inert. Restored on bank when the site respawns.
3. **Wreck cache pickup:**
   - Dropped at the death or recall spot, on the ground.
   - Shows its amount (readable at 8 m camera distance).
   - Reclaimed once within 2.5 m.
   - A newer cache removes the old one.
4. **Collector:** an Area3D helper on the walker (mask layer 6).
   - Picks pickups up within 2.5 m, with a 0.3 s magnet pull to the chassis. The scrap counts on arrival.
   - A pickup is never counted twice, even if two collectors or two frames overlap.
   - The collector follows `WalkerBody.body_pose()` from outside the walker.
5. **Recall hold (`RecallHold`):**
   - Holding `interact` for 3.0 s fires `recall_requested(position)`. Releasing earlier cancels.
   - A tap shorter than 0.3 s is not a hold, because a tap within 4 m of the bench enters the workshop (T09/T12).
   - Exposes progress 0..1 for a HUD ring (T12).
6. **Test course (economy_course):**
   - Instances the real valley (scenes/world/valley.tscn), spawns pickups at every ScrapSite, and puts a Scout with
     an OrbitCamera at the workshop site.
   - Scenario helpers: teleport to a site, walk toward a point, die, recall, bank, reclaim.
7. **Valley numbers to report:** scrap per ring and in the pockets (sums from the markers); straight-line walking
   time for a Scout (4.5 m/s) between neighbouring ring 0-1 nodes, against the 15-25 s pacing target; and how many
   expeditions a player who banks every ring 0-1 node once needs for the first purchase (40 scrap), against
   "expedition 1-2". These are inputs for the lead, not gates.
