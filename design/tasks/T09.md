## Goal
In the workshop the walker stands on a stand in the centre with the camera slowly orbiting it. On the left is the
part list: what you own and what you can buy, with prices. On the right is the stat panel. Pick a part and hover a
socket: the socket lights up and every stat shows its change before you place it. LMB places the part (legs go in
mirrored pairs), RMB takes one off back into the inventory. "Exit [Tab]" leaves, and while the build is invalid it is
disabled and says why ("Needs 4, 6 or 8 legs", "Overloaded: 412/380 kg").

## Work items
1. **Inventory model (`Inventory`, pure RefCounted, scripts/workshop/):**
   - It holds owned part counts and starts from `PartCatalog.starting_inventory()`.
   - `buy(part_id, economy) -> bool`: legs are bought as a pair (2 legs for the pair price), other parts singly. It
     calls `economy.spend`.
   - `mount(build, socket_id, part_id) -> bool` and `unmount(build, socket_id) -> bool`:
     - a leg mounts on its socket and the mirror socket together, so it needs 2 owned;
     - taking a leg off removes the pair;
     - unmounting returns the parts to the inventory;
     - a part is never created or lost.
   - `preview(build, socket_id, part_id) -> Dictionary` returns the stat deltas of a place or remove without
     changing the build.
   - Unit tests for all of this, including a 500-operation seeded sequence where owned + mounted parts and scrap
     stay conserved.
2. **Workshop scene (scenes/workshop/workshop.tscn):**
   - **Stand and walker:** a stand in the centre, with the walker (scenes/walker/walker.tscn) on it showing the
     current build. Every change re-applies the build at once.
   - **Camera:** it orbits the stand by itself at 8 deg/s and pauses while the cursor is over a socket or a panel.
     MMB drag orbits by hand, the wheel zooms (4-9 m), the cursor stays visible, and the walker takes no field input.
     These are lead defaults; Klas tunes them at the playtest.
   - **Part list (left):**
     - each part's name, owned count, price and a Buy button;
     - Buy is disabled with "Need N more" when you can't afford it;
     - selecting a part arms it for placing.
   - **Sockets:**
     - hovering a socket highlights it: an outline ring in the player accent `#FF8A3D`, never the threat hue;
     - a socket that can't take the armed part shows a neutral cross and the reason;
     - LMB places, RMB on a mounted part removes it.
   - **Stat panel (right):**
     - shows speed, turn, step-up, climb, slope, HP, DPS, spread, and load as mass/lift;
     - with a part armed over a socket, or hovering a mounted part, each changed stat shows its delta with a sign and
       a ▲/▼ mark, so it reads without colour;
     - no threat hue.
   - **Banked scrap:** shown above the part list.
   - **Exit button:** "Exit [Tab]". While the build is invalid it is disabled and shows `invalid_reason()` under it.
     When valid, Tab or a click emits `exit_requested(build)` within 1 frame. T12 does the transition to the field
     and the 2 s budget.
3. **API for T12:** `setup(build, inventory, economy)`, the signal `exit_requested(build)`, and a `Workshop` script
   that does not assume it is the main scene.
4. **Scenario workshop_edit**, starting with the Scout, its starting inventory and 200 banked scrap:
   - buy a long leg pair (70; banked 130);
   - hover a free leg socket with it armed and read the preview deltas (shot);
   - place it (8 legs) and check the stats against WalkerBuild;
   - remove it again;
   - remove legs until the build is invalid: exit disabled, reason "Needs 4, 6 or 8 legs" (shot);
   - build an overloaded case and check its "Overloaded" reason;
   - restore a valid build: exit emits.
   - All UI input goes through real InputEvents (mouse motion and buttons, Tab), not direct calls.
5. **Readability (tools/pixels.ps1):**
   - Panel text >= 14 px tall at 1080p.
   - The socket highlight ring differs from the walker body behind it by >= 0.25 luma.
   - The part list and stat panel together cover <= 40 % of the screen width, so the walker stays the centre.

## Review round 2 decisions (2026-10-09)
Test-runner: tier 3 PASS on e692cbb (354 tests). Code-reviewer: no blocking findings. Playtest-critic: iterate (the
delta marks direction, not better/worse). Lead calls: the GDD 12 Workshop bullets and the Decisions log (main).
"14 px tall" means cap height (measured 15 px at 1080p: passes). The ring check is "the ring (its ink edge or its
core) differs from the body behind it by >= 0.25 luma".
1. **Better or worse in the deltas** (GDD 12):
   - a filled accent mark for better, a hollow neutral-grey mark for worse, with the sign kept;
   - a polarity table: higher is better for speed, turn, step-up, climb, slope, HP and DPS; lower is better for
     spread and load;
   - a unit test per stat's polarity;
   - a workshop_edit assert: the long-pair preview marks spread and turn as worse and load as better;
   - the two marks differ by >= 0.25 luma, and neither uses the threat hue.
2. **Form shading on the stand walker:**
   - put the workshop ambient back to about field level and angle the key light;
   - chassis top and side differ by >= 0.10 luma, and a leg crossing the chassis differs from it by >= 0.10;
   - the ring check uses the restated wording above.
3. **A blocked exit answers** (GDD 12):
   - Tab or a click while invalid pulses the reason text for 0.3 s;
   - the Load row shows "!" while load > 1.0;
   - scenario asserts for the pulse and the flag.
4. **Exit latch** (code-review 1):
   - after `exit_requested`, the workshop ignores input and stops forcing the cursor until the next `setup()`;
   - test: after a valid Tab, a second Tab and an LMB on a socket leave one exit and the build unchanged;
   - fix workshop_edit's `exits_seen == 2` expectation (re-open with `setup()` between the two exits);
   - document in the T12 API that an unused workshop is freed or disabled, since it brings its own camera, lights
     and WorldEnvironment.
5. **Tests that can fail:**
   - `setup()` twice: one connection to the new economy, none to the old one, labels from the new inventory; emit
     `armed_changed` when `setup()` clears the armed part;
   - the exit hands out the very build passed to `setup()`, never a DisplayBuild;
   - time the click path too;
   - a click on a panel over a socket does not place a part.
6. **Per-frame work:** compute the preview (and the anchors) only when the hover signature or the build changes.
7. **Number format:** whole degrees for turn and slope; a fixed number of decimals per stat, with the delta matching
   the value's decimals.
