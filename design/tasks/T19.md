## Goal
Every time a foot plants, a short dust puff kicks up around the pad and a dark contact mark appears under it, then
fades over 2 s. A walking walker leaves a short fading trail of footprints, so the player sees the gait rhythm and that
planted feet stay put. It works on flat ground, slopes and ledge tops, for any leg count up to 8.

## Design
- **Dust puff:** on each `foot_planted`, a puff centred on the plant point, about 0.6 m across, rising <= 0.3 m, gone
  0.2 s after the plant. Dust tone from the ground family, lighter than the floor by >= 0.1 luma so it reads in
  grayscale. No new hue.
- **Contact decal:** a Decal at the plant point, projected along `-normal`, covering the pad footprint (0.30 x 0.34 m)
  plus a small margin. Dark value: at full opacity it sits >= 0.15 luma below the floor in grayscale. Opacity falls
  from full to 0 over 2.0 s, then the decal is free for reuse. It never stains the walker, drones or the pad's sides
  (a thin box plus `normal_fade`, or a cull mask that needs no change outside the owned paths; report which).
- **Pools:** fixed-size pools sized for 8 legs at the fastest cadence for 2 s; at capacity the oldest is reused. No
  node is created or freed per plant after warm-up.
- **Quiet starts:** no puff or decal from plants within 0.2 s after `build_applied` (spawn, teleport, a build swap).
- **Position:** use the signal's position. The drawn pad can jump at touchdown on tall descents (plan.md climbing
  follow-ups, `max_landing_snap_m`); report any run where the decal centre sits > 0.1 m from the drawn pad 0.1 s after
  the plant, without changing the walker.
- **Headless safe:** runs under `--headless` without errors (scenarios use it).
