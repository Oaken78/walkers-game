## Work items
**Do item 4 first.** Klas felt the game lag while crossing the boulders in the Windows playtest build (2026-10-09).
That matches the round-6 review: walking ticks of 40-75 ms (about 66 ms on the boulder segment), up to about 143
`body_test_motion` calls per held tick. Item 4's targets are hard gates, and the boulder segment also needs a
windowed run (`shots.ps1 -Scenario <the boulder scenario>`) with no frame over 16.7 ms while walking.
1. **Wall test (code-reviewer round 6, 2):** `_contact_is_wall` probes vertically above the contact, which on a slope
   measures the contact's height, not the face's. Cast a horizontal ray toward the contact at feet plane + step-up
   + 1 cm; a hit within about 0.5 m means the obstacle is taller than step-up (a wall). It must classify the 1.0 m
   ledge, 1.5 m wall, 0.8 m block, the boulders, patch A, the talus and the valley's vertical quads the same way
   every tick.
2. **Slide along over-grip ground:** a clearance-ray wall holds the whole pose, so a walker meeting the talus toe
   at an angle stops dead, while collider walls slide. Remove the into-face component and keep the along-face
   motion. Scenario: Scout and Strider walk into the talus toe at 30 and 60 deg to the face; they slide along it,
   never climb it (the round-6 blocked asserts), and keep >= 0.5 of their commanded along-face speed.
3. **Stalls (playtest-critic round 6):** add telemetry `longest_stall_s` (consecutive ticks below 10 % of the
   commanded speed while input is held). Assert <= 0.4 s on every Crawler talus approach and on patch A. Keep
   `longest_hold_s` reported only.
4. **Cost:** a walker-only timer (`Time.get_ticks_usec` around `WalkerBody._physics_process`) reporting p95, p99 and
   max per segment, excluding the spawn tick, plus a per-tick count of `body_test_motion` calls and of all rays
   (wall-probe rays included in `rays_this_tick`). Then cap the worst case: cache the wall probe per tick, skip the
   bisection when the previous tick was held for the same reason, reuse the `best` array (no allocation per tick).
   Target per walker: p99 <= 2 ms and max <= 8 ms on every segment (headless; report the machine). If a segment
   misses, report the call counts and why.
5. **Tilt smoothing:** the resolved pitch is written straight into `_tilt_n`. Route it through the smoothing (or
   resolve against the smoothed target), and low-pass the ahead plane or weight it by its count of valid targets, so
   a target's validity toggling does not jump it. Report `max_resolve_pitch_deg` apart from `max_tilt_step_deg`;
   assert `max_tilt_step_deg` <= 3.5 on the bumps and patch A. Optional (critic): a critically damped 0.12 s spring
   instead of the exponential ease.
6. **Blocked asserts as running maxima:** `foot_rise_above_apron` and `root_rise_over_nominal` (gait_course.gd:595,
   604) are snapshots at the assert step. Track them as maxima over the whole push.
7. **Spawn and teleport:** set `_ref_plane` from the new feet before `_resolve` in `_plant_all_at_rest`, and never
   accept an overlapping pose there. Test: teleport from the shelf onto the apron next to the wall lane, and onto
   patch A's crest; assert not overlapping and tilt within 5 deg of the ground.
8. **Hip rays after an in-resolve pitch:** `_clearance_rise` runs only at the start of `_resolve`; re-check the hip
   rays after a pitch or raise inside the same call.
9. **Valley pockets (new scenario `valley_pockets`):** instance the real valley (T05, read-only) and drive the Scout,
   Strider and Crawler at the ledge pocket's 0.8 m ledge and at the talus. Assert: only the Strider stands on the
   ledge pocket floor; only the Crawler reaches the talus shelf; the others are blocked and recover, with the
   round-6 blocked asserts. If the scenario harness cannot instance the valley, stop and report.

10. **Pad overlap on slopes (playtest-critic, T14 review):** on the talus the Crawler's right-front and right-second
   pads sit about 0.1 m apart and interpenetrate (gait_rig tilted view: legs 4 and 5 merge into one 25 px blob).
   Keep planted pads on one side at least one pad length plus 0.05 m apart on every course segment; assert the
   minimum gap per segment.

11. **FOOTVIS caveat (T14 critic and reviewer):** the log counts sight lines blocked by the world and the chassis
    box only; other legs, hip balls and pad side faces are not tested. Tag it `FOOTVIS(chassis+terrain)` and say so in
    its doc comment, so nobody reads 5/5 as "readable".
12. **Recentring after an abeam view (T14 reviewer):** `spawn_at` in orbit mode snaps the yaw behind the walker but
    leaves `recenter_enabled = false` after `use_orbit_camera(..., 90)`. Restore it on spawn; assert it after an
    abeam segment.
13. **Steer toggle assert (T14 reviewer):** `yaw_error_deg` returns 0 when no source is set, so the gait_rig toggle
    check passes trivially. Assert that `walker.yaw_source` is the OrbitCamera after the toggle.
14. **FOOTVIS helper for T15:** expose `min_foot_vis` (the lowest visible-sight-line count over all pads at the last
    `log_foot_vis`) as a property on GaitCourse, so scenarios can assert it.
