# T11 Look pass: toon ramp, palette materials, world bands, fog and sky

Pillar served: 2 "Legs that read" (feet and gait read against a calm world); readability rules 1-4 of section 10
GDD refs: section 10 "Actors use a toon ramp with 3 bands and a hard terminator"; "Flat colour bands: each rock face
gets a base, one lit band and one shadow band, with no smooth gradients on solids"; "Distance fog in the horizon
colour: 14 % at 100 m, 30 % at 300 m"; "Film grain at 4.5 % opacity. Faint sky mottling at 2.5 %"; "No bloom except on
emissives (threat, salvage)"; rule 4 "The world stays in desaturated mid tones, so actors pop"; the Palette and
World palette tables. Decisions log 2026-10-08: the ink outline is M1; M0 ships the toon ramp without it.

Implementer: visuals-dev
Branch: (filled by /dispatch)

## Goal
The greybox becomes the M0 look. Actors (walker, drones, wreck crate) get a 3-band toon ramp in their palette hues.
Rocks, cliffs and ruins become flat colour bands, and the valley gets its fog, sky, grain and glow numbers. The
readability gaps the reviews found (the dark ledge alcove, the ledge lip that matches the cliff, the soft wash) are
closed. Nothing about movement, aiming or the map's shape changes.

## Work items (in order; 7 is the one to cut if time runs out, with a note)
1. **Actor toon ramp:** one shader (or a StandardMaterial3D toon setup if it gives 3 bands) for the walker body
   `#E6E1D6`, accent `#FF8A3D`, the drone body and ring, and the wreck crate. 3 bands: lit, base, shadow; a hard
   terminator; the base band renders the palette hex. Threat emissive and scrap glow stay as they are (the drone
   wind-up must still ramp 0 to 3 over 0.6 s and read in grayscale).
2. **World bands:** rock faces, cliffs and ruins use base / lit / shadow from the world table (rock `#9B7D69` /
   `#BBA189` / `#6D5548`, far rock `#A58B78`, ruins `#93806E`, foreground rocks `#352D28`). Flat inside a band.
   Ground `#CAB294` with the wash in `#B99F82` as a thresholded flat band, not a vertex-colour gradient.
3. **Fog and sky:** depth fog in `#DACFB6` reaching 14 % +/- 2 at 100 m and 30 % +/- 3 at 300 m by Godot's depth-fog
   formula; sky top `#90A092`, horizon `#DACFB6`; the smoke column soft and fogged again.
4. **Glow on emissives only:** glow on, HDR threshold above anything a lit solid can output, so the threat wind-up,
   bolts and scrap bloom and nothing else does.
5. **Ledge reads:** a lit band on the 1.2 m ledge lip, and light the ledge pocket alcove so actors pop there.
6. **Pads vs ground (report only):** measure pad luma (lit top, side) against the ground and its stripes in
   grayscale in `gait_course__midstride` and `__pitch_low`. Klas decides whether pads need a luminance cue before
   the M1 outline; do not add one.
7. **Grain and mottling:** a 4.5 % film grain over the 3D view and 2.5 % sky mottling. Deterministic under the
   tools' fixed fps (the same frame number gives the same grain), so screenshots stay comparable.

## Acceptance checks

  (body, accent, threat, salvage, crate hues from the section 10 table).
- Shot `look_check__walker_bands` (Scout at rest on the valley floor, 8 m, pitch 20, sun at 45 deg to the view):
  the chassis shows three flat tones; each sampled band varies <= 0.02 luma, neighbouring bands differ >= 0.08
  luma, the base band is within 0.04 luma of `#E6E1D6`; the terminator across a hip ball or bone is <= 2 px wide at
  1080p (`tools/pixels.ps1 -Column`). Same for a drone (`look_check__drone_bands`).

- `valley_overview__ledge_pocket`: the ledge lip band and the cliff wall behind it differ by >= 0.1 luma
  (was 0.277 vs 0.284).
- `drone_fight__windup_gray` still shows the wind-up as a luminance change (report the drone luma idle vs peak).
- Grain: the same shot taken twice has RMSE <= 0.5.

- Every CHANGED and NEW shot listed with one line on what changed. `tools/verify.ps1 -Game walkers--<branch> -Tier 3`
