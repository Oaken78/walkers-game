## Goal
A greybox walker you drive with tank controls on a test course. Its body is a CharacterBody3D built from a
`WalkerBuild` (T01). Its legs place their feet by raycast, step when `GaitSolver` (T02) says so, and are posed by
`TwoBoneIK` (T02). Planted feet never slide, no more legs are in the air than the gait limit allows, and the body's
height and tilt follow the planted feet. Three scenarios measure the M0 risks on a purpose-built course: the Scout's
speed and timing on flat ground (`walk_flat`), drift and the airborne limit over 20 s on 30 deg bumps (`gait_course`),
and the Strider vs Crawler contrast (`build_contrast`). After this and T04 merge, Klas plays it at the gate.

## Contract (T04, T06, T09, T11, T12 code against these names; do not rename)
`class_name WalkerBody extends CharacterBody3D`, the root of `scenes/walker/walker.tscn`.
```
signal foot_planted(leg: int, position: Vector3, normal: Vector3)   # T11 hangs the dust puff and decal on this
signal build_applied
enum SteerMode { TANK, CAMERA_YAW }

@export var steer_mode: SteerMode = SteerMode.TANK
@export var accel_time: float = 0.25        # s from 0 to top speed (linear ramp)
@export var decel_time: float = 0.20        # s from top speed to 0
@export var turn_ramp_time: float = 0.10    # s from 0 to the turn rate
@export var strafe_ratio: float = 0.75
@export var aim_turn_factor: float = 0.6    # turn rate while `aim` is held (GDD 6: -40 %)
@export var body_height_ratio: float = 0.6  # body origin above the foot plane, x mean reach
@export var height_settle_time: float = 0.15
@export var tilt_max_deg: float = 25.0
@export var tilt_smooth_time: float = 0.12
@export var bob_amplitude: float = 0.04     # m, used only if the plane fit alone does not give 3-5 cm
# Gait tunables, copied into GaitSolver: trigger_ratio, step_time_idle, step_time_top, handover_progress,
# hover_delay, lift_ratio (same names and defaults as T02).

var input_enabled: bool = true   # T09 and T12 switch it off in the workshop and on death
var yaw_source: Node3D           # CAMERA_YAW only: the body turns toward this node's global yaw (T04's rig)

func apply_build(build: WalkerBuild) -> void   # rebuilds legs and top-part meshes, plants every foot at its rest
                                               # pose on the ground below, resets the solver. An invalid build is a
                                               # caller bug (T09 blocks the exit): push_error and keep the old build.
func get_build() -> WalkerBuild                # a copy
func stats() -> Dictionary                     # the applied build's stats()
func teleport(xform: Transform3D) -> void      # respawn and recall: place, replant every foot, reset the solver
func leg_count() -> int

func gait() -> GaitSolver                      # read-only use by telemetry and tests
```
- **TANK** (default, GDD 6): W/S drive along the body's facing (-Z), A/D turn, Q/E strafe.
- **CAMERA_YAW** (the GDD 16 risk-3 fallback, for the gate only): the body turns toward `yaw_source`'s yaw at its turn
  rate (same ramp), W/S drive along the body's facing, and A/D and Q/E all strafe. Klas tries both at the gate.

## Rules
1. **Drive.** Target velocity = facing x top_speed x (forward input) + right x strafe_ratio x top_speed x (strafe
   input), its length capped at top_speed. The horizontal velocity moves toward the target at top_speed / accel_time
   m/s^2 while speeding up, and at top_speed / decel_time while slowing down. The body moves on the first tick that
   has input (<= 2 ticks to visible motion).
2. **Turn.** The yaw rate moves toward +/- turn_rate (x aim_turn_factor while `aim` is held) at turn_rate /
   turn_ramp_time deg/s^2, and back to 0 at the same rate.
3. **Height and tilt.** The body has no gravity: its height and tilt come from the feet. Fit a least-squares plane to
   the planted feet (to every foot when fewer than 3 are planted). Body origin = that plane + body_height_ratio x
   reach along the plane normal, eased with time constant height_settle_time. Tilt = the plane normal, clamped to
   tilt_max_deg and eased with tilt_smooth_time. Every easing is frame-rate independent (factor
   1 - exp(-delta / tau)). If the fit gives less than 3 cm of bob at Scout top speed on flat ground, add a gait-synced
   bob of bob_amplitude (lowest as a group plants).
4. **Foot targets.** Each leg has a rest point fixed to the body. Target = the ground hit of a downward ray (mask 1)
   through rest point + `GaitSolver.target_lead(rest point velocity, step_duration)`, where the rest point velocity
   includes the turn (turning in place re-plants the legs: GDD 6). The target is valid when the ray hits,
   `GaitSolver.is_target_valid(rise, normal, step_up, max_slope)` is true, and the hip can reach it (<= 0.99 x
   reach). Feed `foot_errors`, `targets_valid`, `moving` (any move, strafe or turn input) and `speed_ratio` (the
   fastest rest point's speed / top_speed) to `GaitSolver.update` every tick.
5. **Planted feet never move, and the body never drags them.** A planted foot stays at the exact world point where it
   planted. Before moving, the body checks its whole next transform (translation, yaw, height, tilt): if any planted
   foot would end up further than 0.99 x reach from its hip, the body moves only the largest fraction of the step that
   keeps every planted foot in reach. This is how a blocked leg "stops the body on that side" (GDD 8.2): the walker
   halts at an obstacle and can still back off, turn or strafe away, and no IK clamp ever drags a planted foot.
6. **Swing.** On `step_started` a leg records `from` = its foot and `to` = its current target, and follows
   `GaitSolver.swing_point(from, to, progress, lift_height(reach))`. On landing the foot snaps to `to` and
   `foot_planted(leg, position, normal)` fires. A HOVERING leg holds its foot at its rest point, lifted by its lift
   height.
7. **IK and meshes.** Every tick each leg calls `TwoBoneIK.solve(hip, foot, upper, lower, pole)` with the pole
   outward + up, upper = 0.45 x reach and lower = 0.55 x reach (exports on `WalkerLeg`), and poses its upper and lower
   bone meshes from hip to knee to foot.
8. **Greybox look.** Chassis box in player body `#E6E1D6`, hips and feet in accent `#FF8A3D` (GDD 10 palette;
   StandardMaterial3D, no shaders). Foot pads at least 0.20 m tall and 0.25 m across, so they read >= 12 px at 1080p
   out to 11 m from an 8 m camera (GDD 10 rule 1). Each top-socket part gets a placeholder: a barrel for the pulse
   cannon, a slab for the armour plate, so the Strider and the Crawler differ in silhouette. Hips are spread evenly
   along each side by mounted-leg order (6 legs leave no gap at the empty socket). The rest stance fans the front legs
   forward and the rear legs back.
9. **Body collider.** Its bottom sits at or above the hip plane. Body height (0.6 x reach) equals step-up
   (0.6 x reach), so anything a leg can climb must pass under the body. The collider still stops the body at cliffs
   and at anything taller than step-up.
10. **Per-tick budget.** 8 legs, 8 raycasts and 8 IK solves at 60 Hz; no per-tick allocations in the leg loop.

## Telemetry (`class_name WalkerTelemetry extends Node`, observes one WalkerBody)
Public properties the scenarios assert on, all cleared by `reset()`:
- `top_speed_mps` (max horizontal speed), `time_to_top_s` (from the first tick with move input to the first tick at
  >= 98 % of the stat top speed), `stop_time_s` (from releasing input to speed < 0.05 m/s).
- `max_yaw_rate_dps`, `time_to_turn_rate_s`, `input_to_motion_ticks`, `first_lift_ticks`.
- `max_drift_m` (the largest distance a rendered planted foot moved from where it planted, over all steps),
  `airborne_violations` (ticks with `airborne_count() > gait_limit()`), `max_airborne`, `min_steps_per_leg`,
  `held_ticks` (ticks where rule 5 shortened the move).
- `max_tilt_deg`, `body_height_m` (mean over the last second), `bob_amplitude_m` (half the peak-to-peak body height
  over the last 2 s), `max_step_up_m` (set by the course, below).
- `report()` prints one `TELEMETRY {json}` line to the log, so GDD 4's "Speed, turn and step-up are logged" holds.

## Test course (`scenes/test/gait_course.tscn` + `scripts/test/gait_course.gd`)
Built in code from a fixed seed, static collision on layer 1, and one plain camera that follows the walker at 8 m and
25 deg pitch from behind-right (T04's rig replaces it at the gate). Lanes:
- **flat:** 150 m straight.
- **bumps:** 100 x 30 m of seeded bumps whose steepest triangle is 28-30 deg (exposed as `max_slope_deg`).
- **ledges:** flat 6 m, then a 6 m deep block, repeated, with block heights 0.30 to 1.05 m in 0.05 m steps. A walker
  climbs each block it can, drops off it, and stops at the first one it cannot climb. `max_step_up_m` = the highest
  block on which every foot was planted on its top at the same time.
- **wall:** a 1.5 m wall across the lane (taller than any step-up).

Helpers the scenarios call:
- `use_build(name)` with `"scout"`, `"strider"`, `"crawler"`, `"quad"` (4 medium legs and 1 cannon: the wave gait).
- `spawn_at(lane)`.
- `hold_action(action, pressed)` (`Input.action_press/release`; the harness cannot hold two actions at once).
- `record_result(slot)` with `"a"` or `"b"`: copies `top_speed_mps` and `max_step_up_m` into `a_speed`, `a_step_up` or
  `b_speed`, `b_step_up`, and updates `contrast_speed` and `contrast_step_up` ((a - b) / a).
- `show_pair()`: the Strider and the Crawler side by side, walking, for the screenshot.

Node names for `assert_prop`: `%Walker`, `%Telemetry`, and the root as `.`.

## Review round 3 decisions (2026-10-09)
- **Shorter steps instead of a block (GDD 8.2 "Foot targets", Decisions log):** a target too high or too steep
  makes the leg take the farthest valid foothold between its current foot and the target. The leg blocks only when
  none is valid. While rule 5 holds the body, the lead uses the actual velocity.
- **Rule 10 ray budget, amended:** peaks of up to about 24 rays per tick (stepped terrain, landings) are accepted.
  The code-reviewer found every ray path bounded (theoretical worst case 52 for 8 legs). Frame time p95 stays the
  binding check.
- **Leg holds do not reset the walk:** only a real collision zeroes the body's velocity. After a short hold by
  the legs, the body resumes at its commanded speed, not from a standstill.
- **Knee telemetry follows the GDD bar:** `min_knee_rise_ratio` counts planted feet within 0.5 x reach fore-aft of
  rest, in the hip frame, for every build. A new `min_knee_bend_ratio` (the knee's distance from the hip-foot line
  on the pole side) proves on any ground that a knee never flips or straightens.
- **Asserts:** an existing assert or scenario check is never removed or loosened without saying so in the commit
  body and the hand-back (round 2 dropped the Strider's 38 deg recovery silently).
- **Body tilt (Klas, GDD 5 and Decisions log):** the tilt clamp is the build's `max_slope` (Strider 30, Scout 35,
  Crawler 45 deg), not 25 deg. Tilt asserts move with it.
- **Owned paths added:** `test/integration/test_walker_rig.gd` and, for the frame budget, `test/scenarios/gait_slopes.json`.

## Review round 5 decisions (2026-10-09)
- **Pitch ahead (GDD 8.2, Decisions log):** near a slope's foot or a shelf edge the body pitches toward the next
  footholds before its feet get there. In-grip ground contact is resolved by pitching first, raising second.
- **Partial moves:** when a rise would break reach, rule 5 takes the largest move fraction (with its rise) that
  keeps the feet in reach; it never reverts the whole move. An overlapping pose is never accepted.
- **Talus lane:** the course copies the valley talus exactly (`ValleyLayout.talus_y`): flat apron, sharp corner,
  40 deg for a 4.9 m run, sharp edge onto a flat shelf, 12 m wide. It replaces the 40 deg ridge patch. The Crawler
  crosses it from several approaches; the Scout and Strider are blocked and recover.
- **Midstride acceptance (playtest-critic, round 4):** a SWINGING pad on the visible row sits >= 12 px above the
  planted pads' bottom row with only shadow or ground between; planted pads touch their own shadow.
- **Baselines after the merge:** midstride, a_vs_b, steep, crawler_crest_b, and a talus-edge frame;
  crawler_crest_a and _c are retired.
