## Goal
Two proof scenarios the M0 review needs, run on the real valley rather than on test courses: every reference build
tries to leave the valley and cannot, and a fight with four drones on screen stays inside the frame and draw-call
budgets.

## Not allowed

## Work items
1. **map_bounds:** Scout, Strider and Crawler each drive into the cliff walls at a ring of points (at least 24 around
   the valley, including both pocket walls and the down-valley end), head-on and at 45 deg, for 6 s each. Assert: the
   walker's root never leaves the valley floor polygon (from `ValleyLayout`), never rises more than the build's climb
   above the floor under it, and ends each push within 2 m of the wall it pushed. Report the closest approach per
   point and any point where a walker gets stuck (does not back out within 3 s with S held).
2. **perf_4_drones:** the Scout in the ring-1 to ring-2 band with four drones engaged and on screen (pull from two
   encounters if one site holds fewer), fighting for 20 s with the no-lead tracker from drone_fight; then a ring-2 view
   looking up-valley for 10 s. Metrics windows over both; assert frame p95 <= 16.7 ms, max <= 33 ms, draw calls
   <= 1000, live projectiles <= 40, 0 orphans. Report the drones' closest distance to any cliff face.
3. **Shots:** `perf_4_drones__fight` (four drones in frame), `perf_4_drones__up_valley`, `perf_4_drones__ledge_guard`
   (the ledge-guard site's fight in the west wall's shadow; report drone and walker luma against the background).
